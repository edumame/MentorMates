# Code of Conduct

MentorMates adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep feedback focused on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **[contact@mentormates.ai](mailto:contact@mentormates.ai)**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
